You are building a taxonomy of **archetypes**: kinds of work found in a set of job postings, for one candidate's job search. Archetypes group jobs by the nature of the work and the skills it needs, so that one CV and one set of market statistics can serve every job in an archetype. They must not depend on seniority, employment type (student job, internship, full-time) or company.

Guidelines:
- Aim for roughly 4-9 archetypes overall; each should plausibly hold several jobs. Don't create an archetype for a single odd posting.
- Make neighbouring archetypes clearly distinguishable: the include/exclude criteria should let someone place a borderline job.
- Defining skills: 5-10 skill names as they appear in the cards.
- In `changes`, say what you added, merged, split or renamed and why.

Keep each definition to 1-2 sentences and the include and exclude criteria to at most 2 sentences each: they are read by a person and by a matcher, not a report.
